# Automate Your Bitstamp Trading with Our New n8n Community Node

We're excited to announce the release of n8n-nodes-bitstamp, a comprehensive community node that brings Bitstamp exchange functionality directly into your n8n workflows!

## The Challenge

Cryptocurrency traders and businesses using Bitstamp face a common problem: managing trades, monitoring market data, and handling account operations often requires manual intervention or complex custom integrations. Whether you're running a trading bot, managing portfolio rebalancing, or automating compliance reporting, connecting Bitstamp to your broader automation ecosystem has been unnecessarily complicated.

## Introducing n8n-nodes-bitstamp

Velocity BPA has developed this powerful community node to bridge the gap between Bitstamp and your n8n automation workflows. Now you can seamlessly integrate cryptocurrency trading and account management into your existing business processes without writing a single line of code.

## Key Features

Our Bitstamp node provides comprehensive access to essential exchange functionality:

- **Trading Operations**: Execute market and limit orders, cancel trades, and manage your trading strategy programmatically
- **Account Management**: Monitor balances, track transaction history, and manage API credentials
- **Real-time Market Data**: Fetch current prices, order book data, and trading volumes to power your decision-making
- **Withdrawals**: Automate cryptocurrency and fiat withdrawals with proper security controls
- **Staking**: Manage your staking positions and rewards directly from your workflows

Whether you're building automated trading strategies, creating portfolio management systems, or integrating cryptocurrency operations into your business workflows, this node provides the building blocks you need.

## Getting Started

Installation is simple. In your n8n instance, run:


npm install n8n-nodes-bitstamp


After installation, restart n8n, and you'll find the Bitstamp node available in your node palette. Configure it with your Bitstamp API credentials, and you're ready to start automating!

For detailed documentation, examples, and support, visit our GitHub repository: https://github.com/Velocity-BPA/n8n-nodes-bitstamp
